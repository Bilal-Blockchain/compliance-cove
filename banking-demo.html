Know Your VASP

VASP Risking Entity Monitoring

Ready to bank crypto businesses? Before onboarding a VASP as a client, you need to understand who they are, who they transact with, where they're licensed, and how they compare to peers. Chainalysis provides the due diligence data to make informed decisions.

Counterparty Exposure Analysis

Understand a VASP's on-chain counterparties — who they transact with, their exposure to high-risk entities, and the risk profile of their user base.

Licensing & Jurisdiction Review

Verify a VASP's regulatory registrations, countries of operation, and compliance posture — critical for your own regulatory obligations when banking them.

Peer Benchmarking

Compare a VASP against peers in its vertical — see how its risk profile, volume, and compliance posture stack up against similar businesses you already bank.

Ongoing Entity Monitoring

Continuously monitor the VASPs you've onboarded — get alerted to material changes in risk exposure, regulatory status, or counterparty relationships.

Fraud Prevention with Alterya

Alterya

Complementing your compliance stack, Alterya provides a real-time fraud and scam prevention layer. It detects when your customers are about to send funds to known scam addresses — helping you prevent the fraud before it happens, not just report it after.
