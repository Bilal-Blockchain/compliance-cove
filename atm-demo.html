About CoinVault

Crypto ATM — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

CoinVault simulates a Bitcoin ATM kiosk interface. It demonstrates how KYT and Address Screening integrate into the physical crypto ATM workflow — screening destination wallets on withdrawal and enforcing daily limits based on risk scoring.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point you can use.

1

Select Transaction Type

Choose Buy or Sell Bitcoin on the ATM kiosk screen.

💬 Talking Point: Crypto ATMs are high-risk for money laundering. Chainalysis helps ATM operators stay compliant with BSA/AML requirements.

2

Enter Destination Address

For a withdrawal, enter the destination BTC address. Use the pre-screened picker to try different risk levels.

💬 Talking Point: Before the ATM dispenses crypto, the destination address is screened. If it's linked to sanctioned entities, the transaction is blocked at the kiosk.

3

Watch the KYT Workflow

After the transaction, watch the KYT monitoring steps — broadcast, register, monitor, and alert or clear.

💬 Talking Point: Every ATM transaction is registered with KYT for ongoing monitoring. This creates a compliance trail that regulators require.
