EquiChain, tokenized securities on compliant rails

Tokenized equities and funds are regulated securities. Whether they settle on a permissioned or a public chain, the issuer and its transfer agent carry a legal obligation to know who holds the token at all times. EquiChain wires Chainalysis into every step: screen holders before they can hold, monitor every secondary transfer, and map the on-chain landscape, with Address Screening, KYT, Data Solutions, Entity Monitoring, and Reactor.

Why on-chain compliance matters here
  • Transfer restrictions are law, not policy. A security token may only be held by eligible, screened wallets.
  • Continuous obligation. The issuer must know holders at all times, including after secondary transfers.
  • Public-chain reality. Even with permissioning, funds interact with DEXs, bridges, and custodians that must be understood.

Secondary transfer monitoring
When a holder sends the security token to another wallet, the destination is screened before settlement. A transfer to an unqualified or high-risk wallet is restricted, the token never moves. KYT then monitors settled transfers continuously.
