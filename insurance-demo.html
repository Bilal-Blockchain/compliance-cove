About ChainGuard

Insurance Claims Investigation, Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

ChainGuard is an insurance claims investigation console. Adjusters receive crypto crime claims, screen the affected wallet with Address Screening, trace fund flows visually, open full investigations in Reactor, and render a verdict. The demo uses three real, Chainalysis-attributed hacks: the AscendEX exchange hack ($80M, 2021), the Atomic Wallet hack ($100M, Lazarus Group, 2023), and the AlphaPo payment processor hack (Lazarus Group, 2023). Each traces from the named stolen-funds cluster to a named cashout exchange.

Reactor

Trace fund flows, verify losses, create investigation graphs

Screen claimant wallets for risk and sanctions exposure
