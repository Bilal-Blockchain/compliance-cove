About ChainGuard

Insurance Claims Investigation, Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

ChainGuard is an insurance claims investigation console. Adjusters receive crypto-related claims, screen the claimant's wallet with Address Screening, trace fund flows visually, open full investigations in Reactor, and render a verdict. The demo showcases three scenarios: a legitimate stolen-crypto claim, a DeFi exploit recovery, and a fraudulent self-transfer.

Chainalysis Products Used

Reactor

Trace fund flows, verify losses, create investigation graphs

Address Screening

Screen claimant wallets for risk and sanctions exposure

Data Solutions

Cluster intelligence and entity attribution for investigations
