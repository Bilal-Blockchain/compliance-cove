About NexusArena

Gaming / Creator Economy — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

NexusArena is a Roblox/Fortnite-style gaming platform with crypto rewards and an in-game marketplace. It demonstrates wallet screening at player onboarding, compliance monitoring on reward payouts, and withdrawal pre-screening when players cash out their in-game earnings.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point you can use.

1

Connect Gaming Wallet

Click 'Connect Wallet'. Select a provider, enter an address (use the picker). The wallet is screened before the player can join.

💬 Talking Point: Gaming platforms need to screen player wallets BEFORE they can earn or spend crypto. This catches sanctioned wallets at the door.

2

Browse the Marketplace

Show the in-game skins and items. Players can buy with crypto. Each purchase triggers a compliance check.

💬 Talking Point: Every marketplace transaction is a potential compliance touchpoint. Chainalysis runs in the background so the gaming experience stays seamless.

3

Withdraw Earnings

Navigate to the withdrawal screen. Enter a destination address. Watch the KYT pre-screening workflow block a risky withdrawal.

💬 Talking Point: When players cash out, the destination is pre-screened via KYT. If the destination is a sanctioned wallet or mixer, the withdrawal is blocked before funds leave your platform.
