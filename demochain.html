Deposit Funds

KYT — Know Your Transaction

Every incoming deposit is registered with the Chainalysis KYT API as a received transfer. KYT continuously monitors the transaction, analyzes on-chain exposure, and generates an alert if the funds trace back to high-risk or sanctioned entities.

Internal Compliance View — This data is only visible to your compliance team. Customers never see screening results, risk scores, or exposure data.

KYT Workflow

1
Execute Deposit
Transaction submitted to the blockchain
2
Register Transfer with KYT
POST /v2/users/{userId}/transfers — register as "received"
3
KYT Monitors & Scores
Analyze exposure, check counterparty risk categories
4
Alert or Clear
Generate alert if high-risk exposure is detected

Withdraw Funds

KYT — Withdrawal Pre-Screening

Before sending funds, the destination address is pre-screened using the Chainalysis KYT Withdrawal Attempts API. This checks whether the receiving address is associated with sanctioned entities, darknet markets, ransomware, or other high-risk categories — before the transaction is broadcast, allowing the platform to block risky outflows.

Internal Compliance View — This data is only visible to your compliance team. Customers never see screening results, risk scores, or exposure data.

KYT Withdrawal Workflow

1
User Requests Withdrawal
Destination address and amount submitted
2
Pre-Screen via KYT
POST /v2/users/{userId}/withdrawal-attempts — screen before sending
3
Analyze Destination Risk
Check address identifications, exposure, and fraud assessment
4
Allow or Block
Approve clean withdrawals, freeze suspicious ones for review

About ArcSwap

DeFi Trading — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

ArcSwap is a full DeFi exchange demo with wallet connect, token swaps, deposits, and withdrawals. It demonstrates how Chainalysis compliance products integrate seamlessly into a DEX — screening wallet addresses at connection, monitoring every transaction via KYT, and enabling Reactor investigations on flagged activity.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point you can use.

1

Connect Wallet

Click the pulsing 'Connect Wallet' button. Select MetaMask (or any provider). Use the pre-screened address picker to select an address — try a Low Risk address first, then a Severe one to show the difference.

💬 Talking Point: Notice the Chainalysis shield icon — this is where Address Screening fires in real-time. The customer never sees this check, but your compliance team sees the full risk assessment.

2

Review Screening Results

After screening, review the risk gauge, exposure breakdown, identifications, and triggered rules. Point out the 'Internal Compliance View' disclaimer. Click 'Investigate in Reactor' to create a live graph.

💬 Talking Point: This is what your compliance analyst sees — risk level, entity identifications, and the exact exposure breakdown. The customer just sees 'Wallet Connected' or 'Connection Denied'.

3

Execute a Swap

Once connected, use the swap card to trade ETH/USDT. Point out the slippage settings, route visualization, and price chart.

💬 Talking Point: The user experience is identical to any DeFi platform. Chainalysis runs in the background — zero friction for clean users, instant blocking for sanctioned ones.

4

Simulate a Deposit

Click the Deposit button in the Account page. Choose 'Safe Deposit' or 'Risky Deposit' to show both KYT outcomes. Watch the workflow steps animate.

💬 Talking Point: Every incoming deposit is registered with KYT as a received transfer. KYT traces where the funds came from and generates alerts if exposure exceeds your thresholds.

5

Simulate a Withdrawal

Click Withdraw, try a pre-screened address. Choose 'Safe' or 'Risky' to demonstrate KYT pre-screening. Show how risky withdrawals are blocked before broadcasting.

💬 Talking Point: This is the compliance gate — KYT pre-screens the destination BEFORE you send. If the destination is sanctioned, the withdrawal is frozen, not sent.
