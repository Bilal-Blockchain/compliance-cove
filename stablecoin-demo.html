About Meridian

Stablecoin Issuer — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

Meridian is a stablecoin issuer (think Circle, Tether, PayPal PYUSD). This console demonstrates how an issuer monitors its entire token ecosystem with Chainalysis — Data Solutions for in/out flows, cross-chain bridge & swap attribution, whale tracking and market integrity; Address Screening & KYT for the mint/burn compliance gate; and the on-chain sanctions freeze workflow every issuer is obligated to run.

Presenter Workflow
1
Open on Ecosystem + Flows
Start with where the coin lives and how it moves. Scroll through supply-by-chain, holder types, and the In/Out flow map.
💬 Talking Point: "Issuers are accountable for what their token does after it leaves the door. Data Solutions gives you a live map of every flow — across chains, bridges, and DEXs — from one query."
2
Show Whale Surveillance
Walk the top-holder table and the whale alert feed. Click a Reactor link to show the pivot to investigation.
💬 Talking Point: "Concentration is peg risk. We attribute your largest holders and alert you the moment one moves funds somewhere risky."
3
Run the Mint Gate (live)
In section 5, use the pre-screened address picker. Mint to a clean address (approved), then a sanctioned one (blocked). This is a live Address Screening API call.
💬 Talking Point: "You never want to mint into a sanctioned wallet. We screen the destination before a single token is issued."
4
Close on the Freeze Workflow
Section 6: a holder hits the OFAC SDN list. Investigate in Reactor, then click Freeze — watch the audit log update.
💬 Talking Point: "This is the obligation that defines your business. Tether and Circle freeze sanctioned addresses on-chain — Chainalysis is how they know which ones."
