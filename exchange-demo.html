About VaultX

Exchange Onboarding — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

VaultX simulates a centralized exchange new-user onboarding flow. It walks through account creation, KYC verification, wallet linking with Address Screening, and a first deposit monitored by KYT. This is the demo to use when a prospect is a crypto exchange building their compliance program.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point.

1

Create Account

Fill in the sign-up form. Click 'Create Account'. This simulates standard user registration.

💬 Talking Point: This is the normal onboarding flow your customers see. No compliance friction at this stage — we screen when crypto enters the picture.

2

Identity Verification (KYC)

The demo simulates a photo ID upload and selfie verification. Click through the steps.

💬 Talking Point: KYC is your first layer. Chainalysis adds the second layer — on-chain risk assessment that KYC alone can't provide.

3

Link External Wallet

Enter a wallet address (use the pre-screened picker). The address is screened via the Chainalysis Address Screening API. Review the results.

💬 Talking Point: This is where Chainalysis integrates. Before this wallet can deposit or withdraw, we screen it for sanctions, darknet, ransomware, and 30+ other risk categories.

4

Make First Deposit

Simulate a deposit. Watch the KYT workflow — the transaction is registered, monitored, and either cleared or flagged.

💬 Talking Point: Every deposit is registered with KYT. The customer sees 'Deposit Received'. Your compliance team sees the full exposure analysis.
