About Peerly

P2P Marketplace, Compliance Cove Demo. A peer to peer trading platform that shows the full Chainalysis compliance journey, from counterparty screening to escrow monitoring to post release investigation.

What This Demo Shows

Peerly lets people trade crypto directly with each other while the platform holds funds in escrow. It demonstrates how Chainalysis screens each counterparty before a trade, monitors the escrow deposit and payout with KYT, and lets a compliance analyst investigate flagged trades in Reactor. The continuous monitoring scenario shows how KYT catches risk that a point in time screen would miss.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point.

1

Pick an offer

Choose a seller from the marketplace. Each offer carries a different counterparty risk profile.

Talking point: In P2P, you do not know who is on the other side. Chainalysis turns an anonymous wallet into a risk decision.

2

Watch the counterparty screen

Try the clean seller, the enhanced review seller, and the sanctioned seller to show how each outcome is handled.

Talking point: A sanctioned counterparty is blocked before any funds are escrowed, not after.

3

Release and keep watching

Run the continuous monitoring offer. It screens clean, then KYT raises an alert weeks later when the funds reach a sanctioned entity.

Talking point: Screening is a moment in time. KYT monitoring protects you for the life of the funds.
