About QuantDesk

Trading Firm / Market Maker, Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

QuantDesk is a Bloomberg-style institutional trading desk dashboard built for crypto market makers like Cumberland, Jump, and Jane Street. It demonstrates how ALL Chainalysis products work together, Hexagate for smart contract risk, Data Solutions for market intelligence, KYT for transfer monitoring, Address Screening for counterparty risk, and Reactor for investigations.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point you can use.

1

Trading Desk, Portfolio Overview

Start on Trading Desk. Show active positions across venues, P&L, and recent cross-venue transfers with KYT status badges.

💬 Talking Point: This is your trading desk. Every position, every venue, every transfer, all monitored. Notice the KYT status on each transfer: cleared or under review.

2

Protocol Risk, Hexagate

Switch to Protocol Risk. Show the protocols the firm provides liquidity to, each with a Hexagate risk badge and monitoring status.

💬 Talking Point: You're providing $500M+ in liquidity across these protocols. Hexagate monitors every smart contract you interact with, if an exploit pattern is detected, you know before it hits.

3

Token Due Diligence, Hexagate + Data Solutions

Switch to Token Intel. Pick a stablecoin (USDC is the default). Walk through the three live checks: contract security (Hexagate), on-chain activity (Gate DSL), and risk exposure (Data Solutions). Point out the verdict card at the bottom.

💬 Talking Point: Before making markets in any token, your compliance team runs this check. In seconds you know: is the contract safe, is it actively traded, and what percentage of volume touches risky counterparties. Right now, USDT shows 10x the sanctions exposure of USDC. That changes how you size your book and what monitoring you put in place.

4

Market Intel, Data Solutions

Switch to Market Intel. Walk through exchange flows, LP monitoring, whale tracking, and the live Token Risk Profiles table (bottom left, powered by Data Solutions).

💬 Talking Point: This is where Data Solutions shines. Exchange inflows tell you where capital is moving. Whale tracking shows who is accumulating. The Token Risk Profiles table pulls live from Data Solutions to show which stablecoins carry the most compliance risk.

5

Counterparty Screening

Switch to Counterparty. Show the auto-screened counterparties and the 'Screen New Counterparty' form.

💬 Talking Point: Every wallet your firm trades with on-chain is auto-screened via Address Screening. 847 cleared, 12 under review, 3 blocked, this is your compliance gate for OTC deals and LP partnerships.

6

Compliance Reporting

Switch to Compliance. Show the activity log, screening volume chart, and the products used badge row.

💬 Talking Point: This is what your compliance officer shows to regulators. 862 addresses screened, 8 alerts generated, 100% resolved. Five Chainalysis products, one compliance program.
