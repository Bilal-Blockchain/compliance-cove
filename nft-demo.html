About NovaMint

Digital Marketplace — Compliance Cove Demo

What This Demo Shows

NovaMint is an NFT marketplace where users connect wallets to mint, buy, and trade digital collectibles. Address Screening gates wallet connections — sanctioned wallets are blocked before they can interact with the marketplace.

Presenter Workflow

Follow these steps during a customer call. Each step includes a talking point you can use.

1

Connect Wallet

Click 'Connect Wallet' in the nav. Select a provider, enter an address. The wallet is screened via Address Screening.

💬 Talking Point: Before any wallet can mint, buy, or list NFTs, it's screened. This prevents sanctioned entities from laundering money through your marketplace.

2

Browse Collections

Show the trending collections, notable drops, and marketplace activity. This is the normal user experience.

💬 Talking Point: The marketplace looks and feels like OpenSea. Compliance happens invisibly — users never know they were screened.

3

Review Screening Results

After connecting, review the risk gauge and exposure breakdown. Try a Severe address to show a blocked wallet.

💬 Talking Point: When a sanctioned wallet tries to connect, they see 'Connection Denied'. Your compliance team sees the full risk assessment and can investigate further in Reactor.
